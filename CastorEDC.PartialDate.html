    CastorEDC.PartialDate (ExCastorEDC v1.0.0-rc.1)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    A date whose day, or day and month, may be unknown.

    Fields of type date_partial store unknown parts as 00, such as "00-05-2020" for May 2020. CastorEDC.FieldValue.decode/2 turns their values into this struct, with nil for the parts that are unknown, whether or not the date is complete.
